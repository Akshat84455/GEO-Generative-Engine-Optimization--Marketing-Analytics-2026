# GEO & Share of Model: The New Frontier of Marketing Analytics
**Author:** Akshat Bhatt | MBA in Marketing & Business Analytics

## Project Overview
This project explores the shift from traditional SEO to **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**. Using Google's NotebookLM, I synthesized 2026 industry trends to build a strategic framework for brands to capture "Share of Model" in AI-driven search environments like SearchGPT and Perplexity.

## Key Insights
* **The Death of the Click:** Why Gartner predicts a 25% drop in search volume by 2026.
* **Share of Model (SoM):** A new quantitative KPI for measuring brand visibility within LLMs.
* **Optimization Tactics:** Implementing machine-readable content and digital consensus.

## Tools Used
* **NotebookLM:** Knowledge synthesis and video generation.
* **ArXiv Research:** Source for the technical GEO framework.
* **Gartner/McKinsey:** For market forecasting data.
